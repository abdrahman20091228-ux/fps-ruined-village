# Asset Credits — *Ashes of Vale*

All third-party assets used in this project are CC0 / Public Domain or permissive MIT / ISC open-source assets, decimated, re-packed, and compressed for fast mobile web delivery (`88` manifest files, `4.45 MB`; `4.55 MB` total repository package).

## 3D Models (`assets/models/` — `3.23 MB`)
- **First-Person & World Weapons (`m4.glb`, `akm.glb`, `pistol.glb`, `holo.glb`, `world_m4.glb`, `world_akm.glb`, `world_pistol.glb`)**: *Pichuliru / nisu* CC0 FPS weapon pack (`ar_m4_hd`, `ar_ak_hd`, `pistol_west`, EOTech holographic sight), Meshopt-compressed with 512px WebP textures, articulated armored gloved first-person arms, tritium sights, and `gunmetal_detail` / `wood` PBR-baked overlays.
- **Enemy Tactical Soldiers (`enemy_fighter.glb`, `enemy_swat.glb`, `enemy_soldier.glb`, `enemy_militia.glb`)**: *Quaternius* CC0 *Ultimate Modular Men & Women / SWAT / Soldier / Worker* rigged characters with native `CharacterArmature|*` combat, hit reaction, and grounded collapse animations, box-projected `camo_fabric` / `glove_leather` tactical uniforms, and `SkeletonUtils.clone` world weapon attachments.
- **Street Props & Vehicles (`wreck_car.glb`, `wreck_pickup.glb`, `wreck_sedan.glb`, `road_barrier.glb`, `mil_crate.glb`, `barrel.glb`, `ammo_box.glb`, `street_lamp.glb`, `old_tyre.glb`, `generator.glb`, `wooden_table.glb`, `jerrycan.glb`, `medical_box.glb`, `rollershutter.glb`)**: Poly Haven CC0 3D models & burnt vehicle wrecks, albedo-baked/resized to ≤512px WebP and Meshopt-compressed.
- **Woodland Nature (`dead_tree1.glb`, `dead_tree1_lod.glb`, `dead_tree2.glb`, `dead_tree2_lod.glb`, `bush.glb`, `grass.glb`, `rock.glb`)**: *Quaternius* CC0 *Ultimate Nature Pack* (European trees & LOD1 crowns, bushes, foliage tufts, boulders), enhanced with procedural broadleaf & Mediterranean cypress canopies (`bark`, `leaf_cluster`).

## Textures (`assets/textures/` — `0.63 MB`)
- **Poly Haven CC0 Architectural & Natural Materials**: Packed with Diffuse + AO shading into compact 256–512px WebP maps: `plaster` (`cracked_concrete_wall`), `brick` (`brick_moss_001`), `concrete` (`concrete_wall_008`), `asphalt` (`cobblestone_floor_04`), `dirt` (`aerial_grass_rock`), `roof_tiles` (`grey_roof_tiles_02`), `terracotta` (`clay_roof_tiles_02`), `corrugated_metal` (`corrugated_iron_02`), `bark` (`bark_brown_02`), `cliff_rock` (`cliff_side`), `wood` (`wood_planks`), `metal` / `gunmetal_detail` (`rust_coarse_01`), `glove_leather` (`brown_leather`), and `camo_fabric` (multicam woodland overlay on `denim_fabric_03` weave).
- **Atmospheric & Baked Maps (`sky_dusk.webp`, `leaf_cluster.webp`, `smoke_puff.webp`, `lightmap_ground.webp`, `shadow_blob.webp`)**: Golden-hour twilight equirectangular sky dome, cut-out olive/oak leaf clusters, smoke plume atlas, 1024px offline NumPy raytraced terrain GI lightmap, and soft contact shadow blob.

## Audio (`assets/audio/` — `0.16 MB`)

- **Reload, Doors & Wind Ambience (`reload.ogg`, `empty_click.ogg`, `door_open.ogg`, `impact_concrete.ogg`, `impact_wood.ogg`, `impact_metal.ogg`, `wind_ambience.ogg`)**: Freesound CC0 recordings (`SamsterBirdies #363168`, `qubodup #842748`, `BlondPanda #778462`, `Vrymaa #734669`, `craigsmith #486294`, `aust_paul #30932`, `NickTayloe #830253`), trimmed & transcoded to mono 48–64 kbps OGG/Opus.
