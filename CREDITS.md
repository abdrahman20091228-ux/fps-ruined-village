# Asset Credits — *Ashes of Vale*

All third-party assets used in this project are CC0 / Public Domain or MIT-licensed open-source assets, compressed and normalized for mobile web delivery.

## 3D Models (`assets/models/`)
- **Weapons (`pistol.glb`, `akm.glb`, `m4.glb`)**: *Flat Guns West* & *Flat Guns East* via `petroulacl/fps-asset-kit` (OpenGameArt CC0, Meshopt-compressed).
- **Enemy Combatant (`soldier.glb`, `soldier_lod1.glb`, `soldier_lod2.glb`)**: Three.js example `Soldier.glb` (MIT, rigged character with Idle/Walk/Run animations, simplified LODs + Meshopt/WebP compressed).
- **Vehicle Wreck & Military Props (`wreck_car.glb`, `road_barrier.glb`, `mil_crate.glb`, `barrel.glb`, `ammo_box.glb`)**: Poly Haven CC0 3D models (`covered_car`, `concrete_road_barrier`, `old_military_crate`, `Barrel_01`, `ammo_box`), decimated and Meshopt/WebP compressed.
- **Dead Trees & Ruin Backdrop Shells (`dead_tree1.glb`, `dead_tree2.glb`, `ruin_shell1.glb`, `ruin_shell2.glb`)**: Quaternius CC0 *Nature Pack* & *Buildings Pack 2* (via `trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase`), decimated and Meshopt compressed.

## Textures (`assets/textures/`)
- **Seamless Tileable Surface Maps (`concrete.webp`, `brick.webp`, `plaster.webp`, `wood.webp`, `dirt.webp`, `metal.webp`, `roof_tiles.webp`)**: Generated specifically for *Ashes of Vale* under the overcast late-afternoon palette in `ART_BIBLE.md`, edge-blended for 0.0-delta 3×3 seamless tiling, and encoded to `512px`–`1024px` WebP.

## Audio (`assets/audio/`)
- **Firearms & Footsteps (`pistol_fire.ogg`, `akm_fire.ogg`, `m4_fire.ogg`, `step_sand1/2.ogg`, `step_stone1/2.ogg`)**: *Prepared SFX Library* (1911, AK-47, AR-15) & *Fantozzi Footsteps* via `petroulacl/fps-asset-kit` (CC0), transcoded to mono 64 kbps OGG/Opus.
- **Foley, Impacts & Wind Ambience (`reload.ogg`, `empty_click.ogg`, `door_open.ogg`, `impact_concrete.ogg`, `impact_wood.ogg`, `impact_metal.ogg`, `wind_ambience.ogg`)**: Freesound CC0 recordings (`SamsterBirdies #363168`, `qubodup #842748`, `BlondPanda #778462`, `Vrymaa #734669`, `craigsmith #486294`, `aust_paul #30932`, `NickTayloe #830253`), trimmed & transcoded to mono 48–64 kbps OGG/Opus.
