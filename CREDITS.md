# Asset Credits — *Ashes of Vale*

All third-party assets used in this project are CC0 / Public Domain or permissive MIT / ISC open-source assets, decimated, re-packed, and compressed for fast mobile web delivery (`73` manifest files, `5.05 MB`; `83` total repository files, `5.09 MB`).

## 3D Models (`assets/models/` — `3.23 MB`)
- **First-Person & World Weapons (`m4.glb`, `akm.glb`, `pistol.glb`, `holo.glb`, `world_m4.glb`, `world_akm.glb`, `world_pistol.glb`)**: *Pichuliru / nisu* CC0 FPS weapon pack (`ar_m4_hd`, `ar_ak_hd`, `pistol_west`, EOTech holographic sight), Meshopt-compressed with 512px WebP textures and paired with articulated tactical arms and tritium iron sights.
- **Enemy Tactical Soldiers (`enemy_fighter.glb`, `enemy_swat.glb`, `enemy_soldier.glb`, `enemy_militia.glb`)**: *Quaternius* CC0 *Ultimate Modular Men & Women / SWAT / Soldier / Worker* rigged characters with 10 native `CharacterArmature|*` combat, hit reaction, and grounded collapse animations plus `SkeletonUtils.clone` world weapon attachments.
- **Street Props & Vehicles (`wreck_car.glb`, `wreck_pickup.glb`, `wreck_sedan.glb`, `road_barrier.glb`, `mil_crate.glb`, `barrel.glb`, `ammo_box.glb`, `street_lamp.glb`, `old_tyre.glb`, `generator.glb`, `wooden_table.glb`, `jerrycan.glb`, `medical_box.glb`, `rollershutter.glb`)**: Poly Haven CC0 3D models & burnt vehicle wrecks, albedo-baked/resized to <=512px WebP and Meshopt-compressed.
- **Woodland Nature (`dead_tree1.glb`, `dead_tree1_lod.glb`, `dead_tree2.glb`, `dead_tree2_lod.glb`, `bush.glb`, `grass.glb`, `rock.glb`)**: *Quaternius* CC0 *Ultimate Nature Pack* (European Pine Trees + matched-silhouette LOD1 crowns, Bushes, Extruded Grass Tufts, Boulders), Meshopt-compressed at real metric scale.

## Textures (`assets/textures/` — `1.42 MB`)
- **Woodland & Town Surfaces (`dirt.webp`, `asphalt.webp`, `plaster.webp`, `roof_tiles.webp`)**: Seamless 768px WebP woodland muddy earth/moss, wet cobblestone pavement, weathered European stucco, and charcoal slate shingle maps.
- **Architectural Accents (`concrete.webp`, `brick.webp`, `wood.webp`, `metal.webp`)**: Seamless 3×3 edge-blended textures (`512px–1024px` WebP).

## Audio (`assets/audio/` — `0.15 MB`)
- **Firearms, Impacts & Player Foley (`pistol_fire.ogg`, `akm_fire.ogg`, `m4_fire.ogg`, `step_sand1/2.ogg`, `step_stone1/2.ogg`, `jump.ogg`, `land.ogg`, `player_hurt.ogg`, `bullet_whiz.ogg`, `weapon_switch.ogg`)**: *Prepared SFX Library* & *Fantozzi Footsteps* via `petroulacl/fps-asset-kit` (CC0) and single-shot transient extractions, transcoded to mono 64 kbps OGG/Opus.
- **Reload, Doors & Wind Ambience (`reload.ogg`, `empty_click.ogg`, `door_open.ogg`, `impact_concrete.ogg`, `impact_wood.ogg`, `impact_metal.ogg`, `wind_ambience.ogg`)**: Freesound CC0 recordings (`SamsterBirdies #363168`, `qubodup #842748`, `BlondPanda #778462`, `Vrymaa #734669`, `craigsmith #486294`, `aust_paul #30932`, `NickTayloe #830253`), trimmed & transcoded to mono 48–64 kbps OGG/Opus.
