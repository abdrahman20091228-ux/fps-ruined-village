# Technical & Design Decisions

- **3D Engine**: Pinned Three.js `0.170.0` via jsDelivr ES module import map; zero build step so static files deploy directly to GitHub Pages.
- **Physics**: `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat@0.19.3` WASM via jsDelivr; fixed 60 Hz simulation step (`1/60s`) with transform interpolation and decoupled render loop.
- **V3 Mobile Renderer Pipeline**: Removed the full-screen `HalfFloatType` offscreen RTT post-process pass in favor of direct ACESFilmic backbuffer rendering with `THREE.PCFShadowMap` (`1024px–1536px` focus box), clamped DPR (`<= 1.5 * resolutionScale`), lightweight `32×16` procedural dust-sky `PMREMGenerator` IBL (`scene.environment`), and opt-in `castShadow` strictly on hero buildings/tower/walls/doors and 2 hero street wrecks (`72k–135k` tris, `~76–242` draw calls).
- **Triangle & Asset Budgets**: `renderer.info.autoReset = false`, manual reset/read per frame; hard cap `< 500,000` tris/frame with automatic LOD quality scaling; stripped redundant secondary PBR maps (`normal`/`metallicRoughness`/`occlusion`) and pruned unused props so every GLB sits well under `2 MB`.
- **Village, Verticality & Medkits**: Paved asphalt avenue, sidewalks, telegraph wires, destroyed 2-story homes, enterable **5-Story Apartment Tower** with switchback stairs, and 7 walkable spinning green Medkit pickups (`+35 HP`).
- **Tactical Controls, Hold ADS & HUD Customizer**: Global tap/gesture fullscreen + landscape lock; per-render-frame `consumeLook()` smoothing with `1.5 rad` spike guard Gyroscope (`default 2.6x`); default Hold-to-ADS (toggleable in Settings) with continuous thumb-drag aiming directly inside `#btn-fire` and `#btn-ads`; full interactive Drag & Resize HUD Layout Customizer persisted under `ashes_of_vale_settings_v3`.
- **Viewmodels, Tracers & Dynamic Damage Feedback**: First-person gloved arms for **M4 Holo** (elevated EOTech reflex optic), **AKM 7.62** (detachable `Mag` reload animation + tritium front post), and **M9 Tactical** (3-dot tritium sights); viewmodel strictly hidden outside `'playing'`; fast-traveling (`145 m/s`) additive bullet streaks; 3 pooled peripheral directional damage chevrons recomputed every render frame against attacker world position & live player yaw; compass threat blips (`▲/▼`) and spawn mission banner.
- **V3 Soldier Rigs & Procedural Two-Bone IK**: 4 single-draw-call rigged Mixamo tactical soldiers (`fighter`, `swat`, `soldier`, `militia`, 4.8k–5.3k tris) normalized by live `Idle` Head-to-Foot bone span (`1.62m`), un-crossed `TPose` collar/humerus blend, analytic two-bone arm IK gripping TinyStrike world weapons with closed-fist `Run` finger poses, low-ready vs shoulder aiming, shoot kickback, hit flinch, cover strafing, and smooth corpse fall collapse.
- **Wide Stereo 3D Audio**: Web Audio `HRTF` 3D panning on world sources, 2-channel decorrelated stereo indoor `ConvolverNode` impulse response, offset L/R outdoor building slapback echoes, stereo weapon layering (`StereoPannerNode`), and pickup audio chime.
- **Total Download Size**: `4.91 MB` across `63` manifest files (`Models 2.62 MB, Textures 1.93 MB, Audio 0.15 MB, Code 0.20 MB`; `4.95 MB` total repo size vs `60 MB` budget).
